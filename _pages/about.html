I am a climate scientist developing observation- and data-driven approaches to understand how climate variability and anthropogenic warming shape climate risks across timescales.

My work connects climate reconstruction, Earth system variability, extreme-event analysis, and climate services. I use observations, reanalysis products, model simulations, and reproducible workflows to translate complex climate information into decision-relevant insights.

I am particularly interested in:

  • climate variability across timescales
  • climate extreme events and public health
  • climate reanalysis and reconstruction
  • climate data analysis
  • climate services via data products, tools, and reproducible workflows
